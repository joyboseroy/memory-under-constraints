# Kill criteria

Abandon the research claim if, across predefined ranges of drift, recurrence,
load, interference, noise and recomputation cost:

1. the drift-aware analytic model predicts dynamic performance within
   tolerance for every memory mechanism, and
2. no mechanism has a statistically meaningful difference in its optimal
   policy from the others.

The static analytic model failing under drift does not count against the
claim, since that is expected. Condition 1 refers to the drift-aware model.

If this triggers, the outcome is a negative result and a small reusable cost
ledger and simulator. `thought-machines` stays a reference repo and nothing
further is claimed.

Do not extend the claim to "a theory of memory" or "a cognitive architecture"
unless something unexpected survives replication across seeds and cost models.

## Budget

Two weekends from the first commit of the associative memories. If R-H3 shows
nothing by then, apply the criteria above and stop.
